Deep Dives

5 High-Impact
Events to Execute

These five events deliver the greatest return on admissions investment. Each comes with a specific action plan for reach — direct and indirect.

April
Open School Day
  • Student-guides lead tours — more trusted than staff
  • Google Form QR at entry captures every visitor
  • Principal conversation slots via WhatsApp for serious families
  • Facebook event + boosted post 3 weeks prior to district parents
  • Take-home kit: prospectus + fee card + testimonial card
June
Discovery Camp
  • 2-day free camp — art, robotics, drama. Child's excitement converts parents
  • Parents attend a parallel 45-min info session — zero pressure, very high conversion
  • Certificate with school branding goes home with every child
  • Personalised follow-up call within 48 hours referencing child's interests
November
Registration Drive
  • Early-bird benefit: waived fee, priority class selection, or free school kit
  • Personalised call from principal to every warm inquiry family in CRM
  • WhatsApp sequence over 2 weeks: reminder, social proof, final deadline
  • Ask current parents to forward registration link to 3 families they know
January
Campus Walkthrough
  • Slot-based visits of 8–10 families — intimate feels premium, not a mass tour
  • Route: labs, library, sports ground, classrooms — always past your best spaces
  • Have a student (not staff) explain their favourite thing about the school
  • Personalised follow-up within 24 hours mentioning something specific from their visit
October
Cultural & Art Fest
  • Open public entry — cultural events are non-threatening. Expect 500–1000+ footfall
  • Live Instagram reels of performances generates 2–3 weeks of organic social reach
  • Press invite to regional journalists with a compelling story hook about your students
  • Inquiry table near exit (not 'admission desk') staffed by an approachable team member
Channel Strategy

Events Into
Reach

An event without a communication plan reaches only those who attended. With the right channels, one event generates 3–6 weeks of organic visibility.

💬
WhatsApp & Personal Outreach
  • Pre-event: invitation with RSVP link sent to all current parent groups
  • During: live photo and video updates shared in real time
  • Post-event: 90-second highlight reel designed for forward-sharing
  • Follow-up: personalised message to every inquiry captured at the event within 24 hours
  • Referral chain: existing parents each asked to forward registration link to 3 families
📱
Instagram & Facebook
  • Facebook event created 3 weeks prior — boosted to target parents in your district
  • Instagram reels during the event — student activities, campus energy
  • Post-event carousel: '10 moments from [event name]' — highest engagement format
  • Tag local residential societies, coaching centres, and parent communities
📰
Press & Community
  • Press note to regional newspaper editions with a compelling story angle
  • School data sheet: board results, co-curricular wins, faculty credentials
  • Invite district education officer as guest — builds credibility for press coverage
  • Physical invitations distributed via coaching centres, housing societies, local shops
